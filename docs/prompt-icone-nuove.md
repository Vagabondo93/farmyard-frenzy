# Prompt per le icone dei nuovi personaggi

Da incollare in ChatGPT, nella stessa chat delle icone precedenti se possibile (così mantiene lo stile).

```
Same style as the previous Farmyard Frenzy character sheets: cute but characterful cartoon style like a modern mobile card game,
thick dark-brown outline (#3a2a1e), flat colors with one soft cel-shading tone and a small highlight, warm saturated farm palette,
3/4 view facing slightly right, full body, centered, filling about 80% of each cell, readable at 64×64 px,
same light from the top-left, big head, expressive eyes, short limbs.
Fully TRANSPARENT background: no ground, no scenery, no frame, no shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers.
Avoid strongly blue or red clothing (the game recolors the teams in blue and red).

Make a SHEET of 6 icons in a 3×2 grid, well spaced, transparent background, no labels.
When I write "next", make the next sheet in exactly the same style.

SHEET A
1. Owl (Gufo) – a round brown-and-cream barn owl with huge wise eyes and ear tufts, wings slightly open, gliding, sharp talons forward.
2. Skunk (Puzzola) – a black skunk with a bold white stripe and a big fluffy raised tail, cheeky grin, a small greenish stink cloud puffing behind it.
3. Carpenter (Carpentiera) – a sturdy woman carpenter with rolled-up sleeves, tool belt, hard hat or headscarf, carrying a wooden plank on her shoulder and a hammer in her hand, confident smile.
4. Beaver (Castoro) – a brown beaver with big orange front teeth and a flat paddle tail, holding a small log, proud builder look (NOT a mole).
5. Hare (Lepre) – a long-eared brown hare mid-sprint, very fast and cocky, legs stretched out, with a tiny sleepy "zzz" detail near one ear.
6. Tortoise (Tartaruga) – a determined old tortoise with a thick domed green-brown shell, steady step forward, serious stubborn face, a little scarf.

SHEET B
7. Postwoman (Postina) – a cheerful young postwoman on a small bicycle, mail cap, satchel full of letters across her chest, pedaling fast, one hand waving a letter.
8. Hunter (Cacciatrice) – a woman hunter in a green feathered hat and hunting vest, with a braid, holding a rifle diagonally ACROSS HER CHEST (the rifle must never cover her face), focused look.
9. Fence (Staccionata) – a short section of rustic wooden fence with three posts and two crossbars, a few nails, slightly weathered.
10. Stink cloud – a small round greenish-yellow cartoon cloud with swirly wavy lines (it's the skunk's gas), no face.
11. Bridge – a small wooden plank bridge seen from 3/4 view, with rope rails (built by the beaver).
12. Nest egg tray – leave this cell EMPTY.
```

Cosa mandarmi: i due fogli così come escono. I numeri 1–9 diventano le icone di carte, market e campo;
10 e 11 servono per disegnare nube e ponte sul campo.
