# Prompt per rifare sei icone a figura intera

Poliziotto, Bracconiere, Coltivatore, Benefattore, Apicoltore e Trombettiere sono usciti tagliati sotto la vita.
Da incollare in ChatGPT, se possibile nella stessa chat delle icone precedenti.

```
Same style as the previous Farmyard Frenzy character sheets: cute but characterful cartoon style like a modern mobile card game,
thick dark-brown outline (#3a2a1e), flat colors with one soft cel-shading tone and a small highlight, warm saturated farm palette,
3/4 view facing slightly right, same light from the top-left, big head, expressive eyes, short limbs.
IMPORTANT: FULL BODY from head to feet — legs and boots must be completely visible, nothing cropped at the bottom or at the sides.
Each character stands on its own, centered in its cell with empty space all around (about 10% margin on every side).
Fully TRANSPARENT background: no ground, no scenery, no frame, no shadow, no text, no letters, no numbers.
Avoid strongly blue or red clothing (the game recolors the teams in blue and red).
Keep each character's look exactly as in the previous sheets, only show the whole figure.

Make a SHEET of 6 icons in a 3×2 grid, well spaced, transparent background, no labels.

1. Police Officer (Poliziotto) – the same cartoon officer with peaked cap, dark green uniform, whistle in his mouth, outstretched hand signalling "stop", handcuffs in the other hand; full body with trousers and black boots, standing firmly.
2. Poacher (Bracconiere) – the same shady poacher with patched hood and stubble, holding a jaw trap; full body with ragged trousers and muddy boots, crouching slightly as if sneaking.
3. Grower (Coltivatore) – the same smiling farmer with straw hat, overalls and red neckerchief, a hoe in one hand and a sprout in the other; full body with overalls down to his work boots.
4. Benefactor (Benefattore) – the same plump rich gentleman with top hat, monocle and moustache, holding a bag of gold coins and tossing a few coins; full body with long coat, trousers and shiny shoes.
5. Beekeeper (Apicoltore) – the same bearded beekeeper in a white suit and veiled hat, yellow gloves, holding a smoking bee smoker, with a few smiling bees around him; full body with white trousers tucked into boots.
6. Bugler (Trombettiere) – the same little military bugler with plumed cap and cream uniform, blowing a brass bugle; full body with white trousers and boots, one foot slightly forward as if marching.
```

Cosa mandarmi: il foglio così come esce, in un messaggio nuovo. Lo ritaglio e sostituisco le sei icone.
